Portfolio: two independent galleries

Three layout options for the two-column portfolio. Every card carries a photograph. Images marked PLACEHOLDER are stand-ins from the existing site and are not real completed projects. Titles and tags come from the current project list. Short descriptions are marked as placeholders until you supply the copy.

Option A

Editorial Masonry

Major Works lead with one large feature photo, then a staggered 2-up grid of tall-ish cards. Small Works use a tight square grid that reads as a quick, organised catalogue.
